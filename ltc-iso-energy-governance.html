Certification cycle. Initial audit in two stages (documentation, then implementation), a surveillance audit in each of the following two years, and recertification in the third. ISO 50001 additionally requires demonstrated improvement in energy performance, not only a working system.

Related systems. ISO/IEC 27001 (information security, including physical security controls), ISO 22301 (business continuity), ISO 14001 (environmental management). They share the same high-level structure, so one integrated management system can carry all four.
