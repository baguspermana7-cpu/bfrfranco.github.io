Standard 90.4 — how a design complies

MLC — mechanical load component: annualised cooling energy per unit of IT energy, compared against the climate-zone maximum in the standard.
ELC — electrical loss component: losses from the incoming service through UPS and distribution to the IT input, compared against the standard’s maximum.
Both are compared to tabulated limits in the edition adopted by the jurisdiction; this workbench does not reproduce those tables.

What changed in the 5th edition (2021)

Recommended upper humidity raised to 70 % RH (with 15 °C dew point). New class H1 for high-density air-cooled products (allowable 5–25 °C, recommended 18–22 °C). Liquid classes renamed by maximum supply temperature: W17, W27, W32, W40, W45, W+ (formerly W1–W5).
