Method

  • System costC_i = f_i ($/kW) · P_IT · M_location · M_i
  • M_electricalM_redundancy · M_rack · M_distribution · M_transformer
  • M_coolingM_cooling type · M_rack
  • M_generatorM_redundancy · (1 + 0.008 · (h_fuel − 24)) · M_fuel type
  • M_locationregion factor, or city $/W ÷ 4.65 $/W
  • Contingency(Σ C_i + design + PM) · 5 % simple, slider in advanced (10 % default)
  • TotalΣ C_i + design + PM + contingency + front-of-meter + renewables
  • Energy costP_IT · PUE(cooling, rack) · 8,760 h · tariff
  • Cost per kWtotal / P_IT
