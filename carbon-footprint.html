Detail

--
Scope 1?Direct GHG emissions from sources owned or controlled by the facility. Includes on-site diesel generator combustion and fugitive refrigerant leaks. Measured in metric tonnes CO2 equivalent (tCO2e) per year per GHG Protocol.
--
Scope 2?Indirect emissions from purchased grid electricity. Calculated as Annual MWh x Grid Carbon Intensity (kgCO2/kWh). Market-based accounting allows renewable PPAs and green tariffs to reduce this to near-zero. Location-based uses average grid factor.
--
Scope 3?Value-chain emissions not directly controlled by the facility. Includes embodied carbon in construction materials, IT equipment manufacturing, staff commuting, and waste. Scope 3 often represents 70-90% of a data center's full lifecycle footprint.
--
kgCO₂/kWh IT?Carbon intensity normalized to IT energy delivered. Formula: (Scope 1 + Scope 2) / Annual IT MWh. Industry benchmark: below 0.1 kgCO2/kWh IT is excellent; above 0.4 indicates high-carbon grid or poor PUE. Enables cross-site comparison regardless of size.
--
Offset Cost?Estimated annual cost to purchase carbon offsets for residual emissions after renewable energy coverage. Based on voluntary carbon market price (typically $5-50/tCO2e). Offsets should be verified (Gold Standard, Verra VCS) to count toward net-zero targets.
--
Carbon Tax?Annual carbon price liability based on the jurisdiction's mandatory carbon pricing mechanism. Applies to Scope 1 and applicable Scope 2 emissions. EU ETS price currently $60-80/tCO2e; Singapore $25/tCO2e; many regions have no mandatory carbon price yet.
--
Annual MWh?Total facility energy consumption in megawatt-hours per year. Formula: IT Load (kW) x PUE x 8,760 hours. This is the primary driver of Scope 2 emissions and electricity cost. At PUE 1.4, a 1 MW IT load consumes ~12,264 MWh/year.
--
Efficiency?Overall carbon efficiency rating from A+ (best) to E (worst), combining PUE, renewable coverage, grid intensity, and operational carbon intensity. A+ requires PUE below 1.3, 80%+ renewable coverage, and carbon intensity under 0.05 kgCO2/kWh IT.
