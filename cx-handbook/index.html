How to use this manual

Procedures are numbered steps. Each step names who does it, what is measured or checked, the acceptance value and the record that proves it. Highlighted text marks the values and decisions to remember.

Acceptance values are quoted from the standard named next to them. Where a value depends on the edition, the project specification or the manufacturer, the text says so. The project specification and the OEM instructions govern over this manual.

Figures sit in the section they illustrate; each has a stable link (for example F02-1) and opens at full size from the sheet. Symbols follow EN 60617 (electrical) and ISO 10628 (process).

Worked examples use the MSC-1 example campus (4 × 80 MVA, 20 kV ring, four buildings, 12 halls of 12 MW) or a stated example quantity; they are examples, not project data.
