Standards & Compliance — Topic Hub

A curated navigational hub for data center standards and compliance frameworks: TIA-942 rated infrastructure, Uptime Institute Tier certification, EN 50600 European standard, ISO 27001 information security, and ASHRAE TC 9.9 thermal guidelines. 01 Purpose & scope

This hub is the entry point for all standards-compliance work on data center infrastructure. It maps the major international and US standards to specific design checkpoints, rating tiers, and certification pathways. Whether assessing a greenfield site against TIA-942 Rated-4 or confirming EN 50600-2-1 physical security controls, this hub points to the applicable checklist tool or detailed reference article.

The hub also covers information-security governance (ISO 27001 ISMS) as it applies to physical data center controls — access management, CCTV, mantrap, and audit trails — which intersect with the infrastructure design domain.

03 References & standards

  • TIA-942-B (2017) — Telecommunications Infrastructure Standard for Data Centers: four rated tiers (R1–R4) covering architecture, electrical, mechanical, and telecoms subsystems.
  • Uptime Institute Tier Standard: Topology (2022) — Tier I–IV definitions; Operational Sustainability; concurrently maintainable and fault-tolerant path classifications.
  • EN 50600-2-1 / 50600-2-2 / 50600-2-3 — European data center standard: physical security, power distribution, environmental controls.
  • ISO/IEC 27001:2022 — Information security management systems: ISMS controls applicable to physical data center access, media handling, and clear-desk policies.
  • ASHRAE TC 9.9 (2021) — Thermal Guidelines for Data Processing Environments: A1–A4 / B / C / D equipment classes; inlet temperature and humidity envelopes.
  • ISO 30134-1 (2016) — Data centers — KPIs: overarching framework; individual parts cover PUE (KPI-1), REF (KPI-2), WUE (KPI-5), CUE (KPI-8).
  • ANSI/BICSI 002-2019 — Data Center Design and Implementation Best Practices: comprehensive reference for layout, cabling, power, cooling, and security design.
