01 Purpose & scope

This hub consolidates the sustainability and environmental performance discipline of data center operations. It covers the Green Grid KPI suite — PUE (energy efficiency), WUE (water efficiency), CUE (carbon intensity) — and maps them to the ISO 30134 measurement standard and ASHRAE 90.4 energy targets. The hub also addresses decarbonisation pathways: renewable energy PPAs, on-site generation (solar PV, fuel cells), 24/7 carbon-free energy matching, and Scope 2 / Scope 3 emissions accounting per GHG Protocol.

ISO 50001 energy management system (EnMS) implementation guidance is included as the operational framework for continuous PUE/WUE improvement programmes in live data center campuses.

03 References & standards

  • The Green Grid WP#49 / WP#35 — PUE, DCiE, DPPE definitions; WUE and CUE metric definitions; measurement tier guidance (IT-measured, facility-measured, source-energy).
  • ISO 30134-1 through 30134-8 — Data centers KPIs: part 1 (overview), part 2 (PUE), part 5 (WUE), part 8 (CUE); measurement methodology and reporting boundaries.
  • ASHRAE 90.4-2022 — Energy Standard for Data Centers: mechanical load component (MLC) + electrical loss component (ELC) efficiency targets by climate zone; annualised PUE calculation method.
  • ISO 50001:2018 — Energy management systems: Plan-Do-Check-Act (PDCA) framework for systematic energy performance improvement; applicable to data center campus EnMS programmes.
  • GHG Protocol Corporate Standard (2015 update) — Scope 1/2/3 greenhouse gas accounting; market-based vs location-based Scope 2 method; 24/7 carbon-free energy (24/7 CFE) matching concept.
  • RE100 / EACs — Renewable energy certificate frameworks (REGO, REC, I-REC) governing additionality and temporal matching claims in PPAs.
  • EN 50600-4-1 / 4-2 — Data center energy efficiency and renewable energy factor KPIs under the European standard framework.
