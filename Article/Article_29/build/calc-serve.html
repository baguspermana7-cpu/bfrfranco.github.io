How fast and how cheap is serving? Throughput and cost per million tokens

This calculator is a roofline ceiling for decoding. Each step reads the weights and the KV cache once, so t_mem = (W + B × KV_seq) / (n_gpus × BW), compared with t_comp = 2 × N_active × B / (n_gpus × F). The step time is the larger of the two divided by an assumed bandwidth efficiency, throughput is B / t_step, and cost is (n_gpus × price) / (throughput × 3600) × 1e6 dollars per million tokens. It is a ceiling-based estimate, not a benchmark. Every result has a "Show working" line that prints the formula and the numbers it used.

Basis: ceiling-based estimate, not a benchmark. Sources: GPU bandwidth, memory and FLOPs [S8] [S9] [S12] [S13] [S14]; power per GPU [S70]. Bandwidth efficiency, prefill utilisation and GPU price are assumptions. Constants as of 2026-10-16.
