How much does training cost? Compute, GPU-hours and energy

This calculator turns a parameter count and a token count into total compute, GPU-hours, wall-clock days, dollars and electricity. Compute is C = 6 × N × D; GPU-hours are C / (peak × MFU) / 3600; cost is GPU_hours / goodput × price; facility energy is IT energy times PUE. For a mixture-of-experts model enter the active parameters. The formula leaves out attention FLOPs and does not describe reinforcement-learning post-training. Every result has a "Show working" line that prints the formula and the numbers it used.

Basis: estimate. Sources: 6ND [S7]; Chinchilla check [S5] [S6]; MFU and goodput [S2]; GPU peaks [S8] [S9] [S13]; power per GPU [S70] [S12] [S68]. Price per GPU-hour is your assumption. Constants as of 2026-10-16.
