Will it fit? Model memory and KV cache

This calculator adds the weights, the key-value (KV) cache that grows with context and batch, and a runtime overhead, then tells you the smallest hardware that holds it. Weights are N_total × precision / 8; the KV cache is 2 × layers × kv_heads × d_head × kv_bits/8 bytes per token (MLA models use layers × mla_width × kv_bits/8), times context and batch; the total is (W_bytes + KV_bytes) × (1 + overhead). The 10% overhead is a rule of thumb, and the Apple 75% usable-memory share is an assumption. Every result has a "Show working" line that prints the formula and the numbers it used.
