What will this document cost? Tokens and API price

This calculator converts a word count into tokens for a chosen tokenizer, then prices the input and a proportional output. Input tokens are words × tok_per_word[language][tokenizer], output tokens are tokens_in × output_ratio, and cost per call is the input and output tokens times their per-million prices, with cached input billed at a fraction. Token ratios come from a sample of one paragraph, so treat them as plus or minus 15%. Every result has a "Show working" line that prints the formula and the numbers it used.

Basis: estimate. Token ratios: self-measurement on one English/Indonesian paragraph [S72] and the vendor statement of 555k words per 1M tokens [S27]. List prices as of 2026-10-16 [S27] [S28] [S29]; prices change, so edit them.
