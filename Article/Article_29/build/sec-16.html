Engineer's checklist

Everything above reduces to a short list of formulas and defaults. Use the table as a take-home card: the formula is what to compute, the default is the number to start from, and the source is where it comes from. Anything marked derived is arithmetic on sourced inputs; anything marked assumed is an engineering assumption you should replace with your own measurement. To check a model against a GPU, start with the KV-cache calculator.

Four numbers cover most conversations with a team that wants to run a model: weight bytes (N × bytes), KV bytes per token and the context length you will allow, the bandwidth ceiling (bandwidth ÷ weight bytes), and the rack kW that the GPUs behind those numbers draw.
