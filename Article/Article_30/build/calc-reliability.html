Run cost and reliability with compounding error

This estimator shows why a 95%-per-step agent fails most 20-step runs and what a verifier with retries changes. Each attempt is good with probability p; a bad attempt is caught with probability d and retried up to k times. It computes q = (1 − p) · d, E = (1 − q^(k+1)) / (1 − q), Ps = p · E and P_run = Ps^n, then cost_run = n · E · cost_att and cost_per_success = cost_run / P_run. Steps are assumed independent, which is optimistic.

Basis: model — illustrative. Prices are list price on 2026-10-16 (Sonnet-class, A29 [S27] via [S59]); per-step success and detection rate are your assumptions. METR horizon gap [S27].
