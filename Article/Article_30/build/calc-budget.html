Tool-loop budget planner

This planner sets max_steps, a per-step timeout and a cost cap before a run, from the three limits that bind: money, wall clock and context window. Every step re-sends the history, so Cost(S) = pin_eff·[S·c0 + g·S(S−1)/2]/1e6 + price.out·S·o/1e6 grows with the square of S. The step limit is max_steps = min(S_cost, S_time, S_ctx, S_policy) with t_step = ttft + o·tpot + tt and S_time = floor(Tw / t_step).

Basis: model — illustrative. Prices are list price on 2026-10-16 (A29 [S27] via [S59]); time to first token, time per output token and the 200,000-token window are assumptions (A29 sections 8.2 and 8.5).
