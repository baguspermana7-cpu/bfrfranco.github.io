Agent or script? A decision scorer

This scorer turns the decision table into a number so you can see why a task lands on script, hybrid or agent. It rates variability of input (V), cost of an error (E), verifiability (Vf), frequency (F) and latency budget (L) from 1 to 5, then computes N = (V − 1) / 4, Risk = ((E − 1) / 4) · (1 − (Vf − 1) / 4), Lp = (5 − L) / 4 and Score = 100 · N · (1 − Risk) · (1 − 0.5·Lp). Below 35 is script, 35 to 65 is hybrid, 65 and above is an agent loop with verifiers; four hard overrides apply afterwards. It is a judgement aid, not a measurement: the weights are design choices.

Basis: model — illustrative. The weights and bands are the dossier's design choices; the first override cites CISA AI-in-OT guidance [S33]. Constants as of 2026-10-16.
