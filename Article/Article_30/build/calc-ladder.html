Verification-ladder coverage

This score shows how much each gate in a pipeline contributes and why stacking independent gates beats one strong one. A gate that shares the generator's model family loses catch rate: d_eff,i = dᵢ · (1 − ρ). The chance a defect escapes every gate is P_escape = Π (1 − d_eff,i) and Coverage = 1 − P_escape. Each gate's marginal value is P_escape(without j) − P_escape(with j). The catch rates are your assumptions, not measurements.

Basis: assumption — user-set. No measured per-gate catch rates were found for LLM pipelines; the factory has run one demo job only [S61], and its gate order is mirrored in the preset [S60].
