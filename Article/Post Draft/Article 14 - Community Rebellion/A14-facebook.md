# Facebook — Article 14: The $64 Billion Rebellion: Communities vs Data Centers

New on ResistanceZero: The $64 Billion Rebellion: Communities vs Data Centers.

$64 billion in US data center projects have been blocked or delayed by community opposition. There are 142 activist groups across 24 states, and on February 7, 2026, Malaysia saw its first-ever data center protest, in Johor, where water demand was 5.7x supply.

This analysis follows the money, the water and the air. Baltimore residents already saw $17/month bill increases, and data centers were 40% of PJM's $16.4B capacity auction cost. It also gives the industry's case: Loudoun County earns $26 for every $1 of services data centers use.

It reviews the government responses, from local moratoria to global policy, then turns to Southeast Asia and the coexistence models that might work. A community impact scorecard calculator is included.
