# Facebook — Article 20: AI Data Center Water Use: Altman vs the Data

New on ResistanceZero: AI Data Center Water Use: Altman vs the Data.

Is the data center water issue "mostly fake", as Sam Altman has suggested? This article checks three of his claims against peer-reviewed research and corporate reports.

U.S. data centers consumed approximately 17 billion gallons of water in 2023, and projections reach 68 billion gallons by 2028, a 4x increase driven mainly by AI workloads. The estimates come from UC Riverside researchers led by Professor Shaolei Ren, published in the journal Joule, and are supported by IEA and Xylem/Global Water Intelligence analysis. And even if every new site used zero water, the installed base of water-cooled facilities will operate for another 15-25 years.

The piece also covers the communities affected, a full verdict table, and what an engineer thinks has to happen next.
