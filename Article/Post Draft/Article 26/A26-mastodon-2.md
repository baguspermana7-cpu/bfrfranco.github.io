The industry's answer to PFAS: switch to HFO-based fluids marketed as "PFAS-free."

The chemistry doesn't cooperate.

Opteon 2P50 degrades into TFA — trifluoroacetic acid. TFA is persistent in water. Bio-accumulative. In 2026 EU regulators (ECHA) moved to classify it as a reproductive toxicant, and EU drinking-water PFAS limits went live.

The industry already called it solved and moved on.

We renamed the forever chemical. We didn't remove it.
