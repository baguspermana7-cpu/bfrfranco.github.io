Under 5% of data centers have the rack density that justifies two-phase PFAS cooling (>100 kW/rack). The rest adopted it because competitors did.

Now they're sitting on a problem they haven't priced.

A field study measured 17% of the charge lost to vapor in a year — ~$9,500/yr off one tank. PFAS disposal runs $8-15/liter; a 100,000-liter system is ~$1M in liability, on no balance sheet.

3M exited PFAS manufacturing in 2025; the industry runs on stockpile.
