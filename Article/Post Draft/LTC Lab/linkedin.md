# LinkedIn — LTC Labs (Standards Hub)

**Char target**: ~1,600.

---

Shipped a standards lab cluster on resistancezero.com. Six pages around
the master `standards-ltc-lab.html` landing — each one a self-contained
working environment for a specific standards regime.

ANSI / TIA-942 topology readiness. ASHRAE TC 9.9 thermal control.
ISO 50001 energy governance. NFPA fire risk. System modelling lab.
Uptime Institute tier alignment.

The brief was: stop reading PDFs about standards. Start running standards
as live workbenches.

What each lab does:

— Surfaces the specific clause / table / appendix that matters.
— Maps the clause to a measurable parameter your facility actually has
  (cooling supply temperature, recirculation factor, redundancy count,
  hold-up time, fire-load category).
— Lets you toggle scenarios live — what happens if we bump the supply
  temperature 2 °C? What if we move from N+1 to 2N? What if we change
  the fire protection from pre-action to clean-agent?
— Outputs a compliance-readiness scorecard you can take to a designer
  or auditor.

What it isn't: a certification engine. We are not handing out TIA-942
certifications. The labs are educational and decision-support, not
authority.

Closest comparable in market: nothing focused on this. Standards-body
PDFs sit on one shelf, paid-consultancy spreadsheets sit on another. The
labs are the bridge.

Tier: PRO. The methodology is documented openly so any practising
engineer can critique it.

If you run a TIA-942 audit prep or an ASHRAE TC 9.9 compliance review
this quarter — the labs are worth a look.
