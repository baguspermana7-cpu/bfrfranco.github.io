Here is something I have seen at multiple data center sites: the spare parts program looks reasonable on paper until something critical fails and you discover the stock was sized against consumption history, not against the actual failure modes that can take down your systems.

Consumption data tells you what broke in the past. It does not tell you the probability or consequence of what you have not seen yet. For M&E equipment with 12 to 18 month lead times and end-of-life risk, that gap can be significant.

I put together a browser-based engine that runs the quantitative models for this — FMECA criticality scoring, METRIC and MEIO multi-echelon positioning, Kraljic segmentation, DMSMS obsolescence tracking, Monte Carlo stockout simulation, and supplier risk index. 27 modules total, with a built-in parts catalog covering 445 components across 102 OEMs.

It was built specifically for Program Managers and supply-chain leads at hyperscale and colo operators who need a structured framework rather than a spreadsheet.
