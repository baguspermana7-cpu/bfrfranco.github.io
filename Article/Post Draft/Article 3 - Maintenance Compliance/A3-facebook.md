New on ResistanceZero: How to Achieve 97%+ Maintenance Compliance in Data Centers.

Preventive maintenance compliance usually plateaus at 70-85% despite training, dashboards and supervisory pressure. This article argues that the cause is workflow design, not technician discipline.

It breaks the problem into five root causes and walks through CMMS maturity, workflow friction, evidence engineering and escalation architecture. A new site often reaches 90%+ in its first 6-12 months before drifting down; the question is why it settles where it does. The applied case is a 15 MW concurrently maintainable facility that moved from 74% to 97.2% compliance over 18 weeks.

The article also introduces a Maintenance Compliance Predictor that links staffing capacity, workflow friction, CMMS maturity and evidence clarity, with an interactive calculator so you can test your own site.
