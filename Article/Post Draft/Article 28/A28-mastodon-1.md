# Mastodon — Article 28

"Compress 300 TB to 1 KB" and "a laptop = a 1 GW datacenter" are both physically impossible — Shannon/Kolmogorov and Landauer say so.

But you never needed the bytes or the FLOPs. You need the *capability* — and that compresses ~4,000× (a 60 TB corpus → ~14 GB model at 1.58 bits) and runs on a workstation. Compute-per-capability halves every ~8 months.

The real metric is Capability Density: intelligence per byte + per watt. Plus a concrete no-GPU recipe to make a local model sharp on your own data.
