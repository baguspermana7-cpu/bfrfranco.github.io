# Facebook — Article 28

Someone asked me: can you shrink 300 TB of AI training data to 1 kilobyte? Can a single laptop match a gigawatt datacenter?

Taken literally, both are impossible — they run into the hardest walls in physics (information theory and thermodynamics). But that turns out to be the wrong question.

We almost never need the raw data or the raw compute. We need the *capability*. And capability compresses astonishingly: a ~60 TB training corpus becomes ~14 GB of usable model — a 4,000× collapse — and runs on an ordinary workstation. The compute needed for a given level of intelligence keeps halving every ~8 months.

So the datacenter never moves into your laptop. Its *mind* does.
