New on ResistanceZero: NVIDIA's $4B Photonics Play: AI Runs on Light.

On March 2, 2026, NVIDIA disclosed two simultaneous investments totaling $4 billion in optical suppliers, split evenly between two vendors, Lumentum and Coherent. This article asks why.

The answer is the copper wall. AI clusters are dominated by east-west traffic, GPUs talking to GPUs. If compute doubles each generation (B100 to B200 to B300) but network bandwidth grows only 50%, cost per token degrades because more GPUs spend more time waiting.

The article explains silicon photonics, co-packaged optics (CPO), the external laser source that keeps the design serviceable, and micro ring modulators at 200G per wavelength. It shows how Lumentum (the light source) and Coherent (the broad optical stack) play complementary roles, and what it means for AI factory design.
