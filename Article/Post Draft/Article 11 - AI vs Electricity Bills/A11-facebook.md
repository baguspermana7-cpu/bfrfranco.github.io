# Facebook — Article 11: AI Data Centers vs Citizen Bills

New on ResistanceZero: AI Data Centers vs Citizen Bills.

Why are citizens asked to save electricity while AI data centers consume megawatts? This article takes the question seriously for the 680 million people of Southeast Asia.

The IEA estimates data centers used about 415 TWh in 2024, 1.5% of global electricity, and projects 945 TWh by 2030. Southeast Asian data center capacity is 1.68 GW today and projected to exceed 7.59 GW by 2030, a 4.5x increase. A single AI training rack of 8 NVIDIA B200 GPUs can draw 80-100 kW, about as much as 80-100 typical Indonesian homes.

The article asks who really pays for new load, runs a 15-Why root-cause analysis, compares what other countries are doing, and ends with recommendations. A calculator estimates the impact on your own electricity bill.
