# Facebook — Article 8: No Incident Is Not Evidence of Safety

New on ResistanceZero: No Incident Is Not Evidence of Safety.

A period with no incidents is often read as proof of safety. This article argues it is only the absence of a signal.

Citing the Uptime Institute's 2023 analysis, it notes that 70% of outages had causes present, and potentially detectable, for weeks or months beforehand. The article covers lagging versus leading indicators, Rasmussen's drift-to-failure (1997), Vaughan's normalization of deviance (1996), and a taxonomy of weak signals, with an average incubation window of 3-6 months before a catastrophic failure.

A composite case shows silent drift in a data center, where each small step is locally rational. An interactive chart contrasts perceived safety with actual safety health over 24 months, and a detection system design shows how to surface emerging risk.
