# LinkedIn — Pillar Pages (Power / Cooling / Fire / Standards / Sustainability)

**Char target**: ~1,500.

---

Restructured the resistancezero.com top-level navigation around five
pillars. Power. Cooling. Fire & life safety. Standards. Sustainability.

Each pillar is a single page that anchors every calculator, every
article, every cockpit, every standards lab, and every research
deliverable that touches that discipline.

The premise: the audience comes to the site looking for one of three
things. (a) A specific calculator they remember. (b) An article they
half-remember. (c) A discipline they want to ramp on from zero.

Search handles (a) and (b). The pillars handle (c).

How a pillar is structured:

— Hero paragraph framing the discipline in DC operator language.
— Calculator row: 3-6 cards for the calculators that live in this
  discipline.
— Article row: the article series anchored to this discipline.
— Cockpit row: which cockpit pages map here.
— Standards row: which LTC labs map here.
— Research row: which `docs/research/` deliverables apply.
— Related-pillars footer: tasteful internal cross-links.

The discipline that made the difference:

— Five pillars only. Not seven. Not nine. SAAS-grid-of-twelve is the
  anti-pattern (design.md §3 #11). Five is the limit before the user
  defaults to search.
— Each pillar uses a discipline-accent colour — power = amber,
  cooling = cyan, fire = red, standards = green, sustainability = neutral
  graphite. No Anthropic-purple anywhere.
— Pillar pages are public-tier. No gating. They are the front door.

If you've used the site enough to have a "where was that calculator?"
moment, the pillars are now the canonical answer to that question.
