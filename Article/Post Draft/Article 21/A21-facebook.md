# Facebook — Article 21: Nuclear SMRs for AI Data Centers: The $10B Atomic Bet

New on ResistanceZero: Nuclear SMRs for AI Data Centers: The $10B Atomic Bet.

Microsoft, Amazon, Google, Meta and Oracle are all placing nuclear bets, and a data center engineer asks what the costs and timelines really say.

Microsoft signed a $1.6 billion deal to restart Three Mile Island Unit 1 as the Crane Clean Energy Center. Amazon locked in a 1,920 MW power purchase agreement with Talen Energy's Susquehanna plant. A single AI training cluster like Meta's Prometheus can draw 1 GW continuously. The IEA projects data center electricity demand doubling from 460 TWh in 2024 to roughly 945 TWh by 2030.

Training runs 24/7, which is why capacity factor matters: solar is roughly 25%, wind about 35%, nuclear above 90%. The article compares SMR technologies, the cost problem, the nuclear-water connection and the global race.
