# Facebook — Article 10: Water Stress and AI Data Centers in Southeast Asia

New on ResistanceZero: Water Stress and AI Data Centers in Southeast Asia.

58% of global data centers operate in water-stressed regions, according to WRI Aqueduct 2024. Now Jakarta and Bekasi are planning 500MW+ of new capacity in areas that already struggle with water scarcity.

This article looks at what AI does to cooling demand and what that means for water. A 10 MW facility at a WUE of 1.5 uses about 131 million liters a year. It covers the Jakarta and Bekasi pipeline, including Digital Edge's US$4.5 billion, 500MW campus, regional innovations from neighboring countries, and underutilized markets where water is less scarce. Market forecasts put Southeast Asian data center investment at up to $30 billion by 2030.

An interactive calculator estimates water consumption for your own project, and the closing section sets out mitigation strategies for water-stressed operators.
