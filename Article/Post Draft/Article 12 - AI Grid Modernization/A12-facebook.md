# Facebook — Article 12: How AI Data Centers Fund $57B in Grid Modernization

New on ResistanceZero: How AI Data Centers Fund $57B in Grid Modernization.

The counterargument to "data centers raise your bill": their demand is also paying for new generation and grid upgrades. This companion to the citizen-bills analysis examines more than $57B in clean-energy investment, and asks what it costs other users.

The scale is real. Amazon has contracted 34 GW of renewable capacity, which is more than Malaysia's entire 35 GW generation fleet in comparable terms, and Microsoft has committed 23.2 GW cumulative, including a $10+ billion deal with Brookfield Renewable. The article also cites $33,500 per MW of surplus value.

It then examines the cost allocation reality, who actually subsidizes whom, a 15-Why counter-analysis and the efficiency gains behind the numbers, and concludes that both narratives are incomplete. An interactive economic simulator is included.
