One thing I notice in a lot of regional DC market coverage: ASEAN gets treated as a single market when it is actually six distinct ones with different regulatory requirements, grid quality profiles, cooling constraints, and standards frameworks.

Singapore has a managed moratorium. Indonesia has no moratorium but a complex grid situation. Johor is absorbing hyperscaler capacity that Singapore cannot take right now. Vietnam and Thailand are building enterprise and cloud infrastructure simultaneously. The Philippines has typhoon risk and grid fragmentation that the others do not.

The ASEAN Data Center Standards Report 2026 covers all six: market size, standards compliance landscape (TIA-942, EN 50600, local AHJ), power and sustainability mandates, cooling challenges specific to tropical sites, and investment forecasts through 2030.

The cooling section is something I think gets underplayed: ASHRAE design assumptions that inform PUE benchmarks in temperate climates do not translate directly to equatorial sites. A PUE figure achieved in Jakarta means something different than the same number in Dublin.
