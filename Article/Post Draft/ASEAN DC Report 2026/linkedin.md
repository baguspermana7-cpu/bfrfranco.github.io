The phrase "ASEAN data center growth" covers six very different regulatory environments, three distinct cooling climate profiles, and a standards landscape where TIA-942, EN 50600, and local authority requirements overlap in ways that are not always consistent.

The ASEAN Data Center Standards Report 2026 is an attempt to put that complexity on one page per country.

---

What the report covers:

Market size by country: Singapore, Indonesia, Malaysia, Thailand, Philippines, and Vietnam — with current capacity, pipeline, and investment data from the 2026 baseline.

Standards landscape: which tier classification framework applies in each jurisdiction, what the compliance pathway looks like, and where the regulatory gaps are relative to IEC, TIA, and Uptime Institute frameworks.

Power and sustainability: moratorium status, renewable procurement mechanisms, grid emission factors, and mandate timelines — Singapore's moratorium and subsequent cap, Indonesia's RUPTL 2025-2034 renewables targets, and the regional spread in PUE regulatory requirements.

Cooling challenges: wet-bulb and ambient conditions by region, the practical ceiling for air economization in tropical climates, and the implications for ASHRAE thermal envelope compliance in facilities designed against temperate-climate assumptions.

Investment forecast 2026-2030: market projections by country, hyperscaler activity, and the infrastructure bottlenecks that are most likely to constrain delivery timelines.

---

The methodology section is worth reading: the report uses public regulatory filings, operator announcements, and national grid authority publications as primary sources. Where projections are modeled, the assumptions are stated.
