# TikTok script — AI Engineering Maintenance

**Format**: 45-60 seconds, talking-head + B-roll overlays.
**Hook**: 0-3 seconds.
**Three-act structure**: problem → architecture → number that sticks.
**On-screen text**: every line.

---

## Hook (0-3s)

**ON-SCREEN**: 54% OF MAJOR DC OUTAGES = POWER

**SPOKEN**: "Fifty-four percent of major data-center outages last year
were power-related. The technology to predict them already exists. So
why did they happen?"

---

## Act 1 — the problem (3-15s)

**ON-SCREEN**: Sensor screaming. Engineer holding phone. PDF manual.

**SPOKEN**: "Predictive maintenance stops at the asset. It tells you
something is wrong. It does NOT tell the night-shift engineer what
to do. That's still a phone call, a PDF, and a WhatsApp group at 3am."

---

## Act 2 — the architecture (15-40s)

**ON-SCREEN**: 4-module diagram (FMECA → Neo4j KG → Random Forest → NLP)

**SPOKEN**: "Here's the idea. Take the failure-mode worksheet, encode
it as a graph instead of a spreadsheet. Layer sensor classification on
top. Wrap it in a natural-language interface. Now your operator types
'what do I do if bearing temp on Chiller-3 is rising?' — and gets the
procedure, the safety isolations, the spare-part list, and the
citation, in eight seconds."

---

## Act 3 — the number (40-55s)

**ON-SCREEN**: RPN = 200. DIESEL MICROBIAL CONTAMINATION.

**SPOKEN**: "Across 109 fault modes worldwide, the highest-risk one
isn't dramatic. It's diesel fuel growing bacteria in a tank. Slow to
detect, devastating when it hits backup power. The graph flags it as
your top preventive-action target. Concept page link in bio."

---

## Outro (55-60s)

**ON-SCREEN**: resistancezero.com/ai-engineering-maintenance

**SPOKEN**: "Twelve engineering gaps documented openly on the page.
Read those first."
