# Facebook — Article 5: Technical Debt Is Operational Risk

New on ResistanceZero: Technical Debt Is Operational Risk.

Technical debt is not only a software idea. In a live data center it is deferred maintenance, design shortcuts, documentation gaps and vendor lock-in, and it compounds like financial debt.

This article uses Weibull failure analysis and the bathtub curve to show why risk accelerates as maintenance is deferred, and offers a quantitative framework that scores each item from 0 to 100 instead of "we think this is risky". The composite case is a 15 MW colocation facility, eight years in operation, with 127 deferred items identified in a technical debt audit.

One finding: for every year without systematic document review, MTTR for complex incidents is estimated to rise 15-25%. A phased remediation strategy and an interactive Technical Debt Risk Analyzer are included.
