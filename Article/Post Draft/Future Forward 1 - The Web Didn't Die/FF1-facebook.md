The web did not die. It split.

Verisign counted 386.9 million domain registrations at the end of 2025, and Cloudflare saw internet traffic grow 19% that year. Google still held 90.89% of worldwide search. What changed is AI answers: Pew Research Center found people clicked a regular result link 8% of the time on pages with an AI summary, against 15% without one.

Our new Future Forward article looks at the web from 2020 to 2035. Pages an answer or feed can summarize lose; products, tools, communities and transactions that people still need after the answer keep their reason to be visited.
