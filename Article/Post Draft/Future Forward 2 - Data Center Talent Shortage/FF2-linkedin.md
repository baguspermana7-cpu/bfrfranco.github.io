# LinkedIn — Future Forward 2: Data Center Talent Shortage

340,000 unfilled data center positions globally. Is it a shortage of people, or of realistic job descriptions?

The figure comes from Uptime Institute's 2025 survey, which also found 65% of operators struggling to hire or retain qualified staff. FF-2 reads the number differently: "unfilled" means no candidates meeting the posted requirements at the posted salary. The average posting lists 12 to 18 requirements, companies reject 85-90% of applicants as underqualified, and the pay gap against cloud and AI employers is 25-40% for equivalent skills. Turnover has risen from 12% in 2020 to about 21% in 2025, so the same roles are listed year after year.

Three forces make it structurally worse. Southeast Asia has 35%+ CAGR and the weakest training pipeline; Indonesia alone needs about 15,000 new professionals by 2028. Entry-level engineering hiring fell 25-60% between 2024 and 2025, which manufactures a senior shortage for 2032. And about 40% of facilities engineers are over 50, with 50,000-70,000 experienced operators possibly leaving between 2026 and 2029.

The article closes with a mitigation playbook sequenced by urgency and a DC Talent Gap Analyzer that models your gap score, headcount delta and annual cost exposure across 12 markets.
