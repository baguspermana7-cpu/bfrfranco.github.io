# Facebook — Future Forward 2: Data Center Talent Shortage

New on ResistanceZero: Data Center Talent Shortage: Hiring, Pay and Training.

340,000 unfilled data center positions worldwide. 65% of operators struggle to hire or keep staff. But is it really a shortage of people?

Our new Future Forward article reads the number differently. "Unfilled" means no candidates meeting the posted requirements at the posted salary. The average posting lists 12 to 18 requirements, companies reject 85-90% of applicants, and the pay gap to cloud and AI employers is 25-40%. Turnover has risen from 12% in 2020 to about 21% in 2025.

It then looks at 12 markets, from Southeast Asia (Indonesia alone needs about 15,000 new professionals by 2028) to a falling entry-level pipeline and an aging workforce, where about 40% of facilities engineers are over 50.

A time-horizon playbook and a free DC Talent Gap Analyzer are included.
