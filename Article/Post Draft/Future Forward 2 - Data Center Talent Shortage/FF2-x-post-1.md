# X / Twitter — Future Forward 2: Data Center Talent Shortage (thread)

1/ 340,000 data center positions are unfilled worldwide. The cause is not what the headline implies. A thread on hiring, pay and training across 12 markets. 🧵

2/ The figure is Uptime Institute's 2025 survey: about 340,000 unfilled roles, and 65% of operators struggling to hire or retain qualified staff. But 'unfilled' means no candidates meeting the posted requirements at the posted salary.

3/ Postings are wish lists. The average data center operations posting lists 12 to 18 distinct requirements. Companies reject 85-90% of applicants as underqualified, then cite the vacancy rate as proof of a shortage.

4/ Pay matters. The gap between mid-tier data centers and cloud or AI employers is 25-40% for equivalent skills, and the article finds salary compression explains 80% of turnover gaps between hyperscalers and colo operators.

5/ Turnover rose from 12% in 2020 to about 21% in 2025, a 75% increase. The same roles are listed year after year, which inflates the unfilled count.

6/ Southeast Asia is the mismatch: 35%+ CAGR in data center growth, the least mature training pipeline of any major market. Indonesia alone needs about 15,000 new professionals by 2028.

7/ AI is closing the entry points. IEEE Spectrum and The Pragmatic Engineer document a 25-60% collapse in entry-level engineering hiring from 2024 to 2025. No juniors now means no mid-career pipeline in 2028 and no senior pipeline in 2032.

8/ Demographics: about 40% of facilities engineers are over 50, and 50,000-70,000 experienced operators may leave between 2026 and 2029. Uptime attributes roughly 40% of outages to human error.
