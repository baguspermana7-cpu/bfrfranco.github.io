A lot of conversations about data center sustainability in Indonesia treat the grid emission factor as if it is on a clear downward trend. The 10-year generation data for Java-Bali tells a more complicated story.

Coal generation in the Java-Bali system has not declined over the past decade in absolute TWh terms. Renewable capacity has grown, but demand has grown alongside it, absorbing the additions. The grid emission factor for Scope 2 calculations is still heavily influenced by coal dispatch.

I built a historical dashboard for the PLN Java-Bali grid that stacks generation by fuel type year by year — coal, gas, hydro, geothermal, new renewables — with a total demand overlay and year-on-year comparison. The data is from PLN AR 2024, RUPTL 2025-2034, BPS Statistical Indonesia 2024, and IEA Indonesia 2024.

It is not real-time, but it is the most complete public-source baseline I could assemble for the Jamali system.
