# Facebook — Article 18: AI Factories vs Traditional Data Centers

New on ResistanceZero: AI Factories vs Traditional Data Centers.

A traditional enterprise data center houses 500 racks at 5-10 kW each, drawing 2.5-5 MW of IT power. The same rack count of NVIDIA GB300 NVL72 demands 65-70 MW.

This article covers what changes when the facility becomes the product: AI racks at 120-130 kW against 10 kW traditional, a liquid-cooling benchmark of PUE 1.10 versus 1.6 for air, and hyperscale liquid-cooling adoption of 52%, up from 18% in 2021. It compares Ultra Ethernet with InfiniBand, follows more than $600B of 2026 hyperscaler CAPEX (a 36% year-on-year increase), and flags the risks: AI hardware lifecycles under five years, and stranded assets.

It also looks at Indonesia's sovereign AI ambitions. An AI Factory Infrastructure Readiness Calculator is included.
