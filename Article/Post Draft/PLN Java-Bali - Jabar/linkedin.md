Jawa Barat carries the highest renewable share on the Java backbone — but the source is not the one usually cited in energy transition discussions.

The Citarum cascade provides Cirata at 1.0 GW and Saguling at 700 MW. The Bandung-Garut geothermal belt adds Wayang Windu, Salak, Drajat, and Patuha. Together, these anchor a renewable generation base that no other Java province matches in absolute terms.

The Jawa Barat provincial grid detail page maps this generation geography against the 500/150 kV transmission ring, with the 20 kV DC-feeder and industrial-intake overlay layered on top.

---

What the page covers:

- Province-level headlines: installed capacity, renewable share, peak load, and reserve margin from PLN AR 2024 allocated baseline
- Hydro plant locations on the Citarum cascade with their transmission evacuation routes
- Geothermal plant positions in the Bandung-Garut belt
- DC operators and industrial intakes with 20 kV feeder connections
- Major substations forming the Sentiong-Bandung-Cirebon transmission ring

---

For data center operators in the Bekasi and Karawang corridor, the Jabar grid context matters because the 500 kV interconnect with Jakarta and the Sentiong ring substation is the upstream reference for anything drawing on the Jabar backbone. The renewable-heavy generation mix also affects Scope 2 emission factor calculations in a way that is province-specific on Java.
