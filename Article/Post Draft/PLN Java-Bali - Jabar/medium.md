Title: Jawa Barat's Grid — Why the Highest Renewable Share on Java Comes from Water and Steam, Not Solar

---

The province with the highest renewable electricity share on the Java backbone does not have the largest solar installation. It has the Citarum cascade.

Cirata reservoir at 1.0 GW and Saguling at 700 MW sit on one of the most productive river systems in Indonesia. Add the geothermal belt running through Bandung and Garut — Wayang Windu, Salak, Drajat, and Patuha — and the Jawa Barat generation mix has a baseload renewable foundation that the rest of Java does not match.

This matters for the data center and industrial sectors drawing from the Jabar grid for a reason that rarely surfaces in site-selection discussions: the province-level generation mix is not the same as the Java-Bali system average. Scope 2 emission factor calculations that use the Jamali system aggregate are overestimating the carbon intensity of electricity drawn from substations supplied by the Sentiong-Bandung ring.

The Jawa Barat provincial grid detail page maps the generation geography against the 500/150 kV transmission ring. The 20 kV overlay adds DC operators and industrial intake points with their feeder connections — relevant for the Bekasi and Karawang corridor where a significant share of Java's data center capacity is sited.

Data is from PLN AR 2024 provincial allocation and the overlay source file for operator positions. The renewable share figure uses PLN's own reserve margin allocation to the province by load — it is a calculated estimate, not direct metering at the provincial boundary.

For anyone doing grid-resilience analysis in the Bekasi-Karawang-Bandung triangle, understanding where the Jabar generation actually sits relative to the 500 kV interconnect with Jakarta changes the redundancy picture considerably.
