An underappreciated fact about Jawa Barat's grid: the province has the highest renewable electricity share on Java, and the reason is hydro and geothermal, not solar. Cirata reservoir contributes 1.0 GW and Saguling adds 700 MW on the Citarum cascade. Wayang Windu, Salak, Drajat, and Patuha cover the geothermal side from the Bandung-Garut belt.

For data center operators in the Bekasi and Karawang corridor — which is on the Jabar side of the grid even though it feels like Jakarta — this has implications for Scope 2 calculations that are usually missed when teams use the Java-Bali system average emission factor.
