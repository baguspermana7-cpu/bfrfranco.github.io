# Facebook — Article 4: In-House vs Vendor: MTTR Reduction Strategy

New on ResistanceZero: In-House vs Vendor: MTTR Reduction Strategy.

Vendor dependency is a hidden reliability risk. Most data centers assume vendor partnerships guarantee fast, competent incident response, and rarely test that assumption until a critical failure exposes it.

This article decomposes MTTR into five phases and shows that mobilization delay is the dominant bottleneck: across 428 critical incident records from a 10 MW colocation facility, vendor mobilization took 45-65% of total MTTR for vendor-dependent responses. The facility had 36 critical incidents a year.

It then presents the ICB (In-house Capability Building) framework and a four-tier capability layering model, which together reduce incident response time by up to 60%. The point is not to replace vendors but to decide which tier of competence must sit on site. Decision-latency and knowledge-retention analysis are included.
