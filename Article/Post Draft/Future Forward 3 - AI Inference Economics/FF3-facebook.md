# Facebook — Future Forward 3: AI Inference Economics

New on ResistanceZero: AI Inference Economics: The Cost of Running Models.

Training builds an AI model once. Inference runs it every time anyone sends a prompt, and it now takes two-thirds of all AI compute.

Our new Future Forward article looks at what that costs. Frontier-quality API pricing fell 92% in 14 months between GPT-4 and GPT-4o, and 99.5% down to GPT-4o-mini. At the same time, SemiAnalysis projects 93.3 GW of inference-specific power demand by 2030, and AI data centers already use about 4% of US electricity (EIA, 2025), up from 2.5% in 2023.

It also asks which AI products survive the economics: GitHub Copilot reportedly lost an average of $20 per user per month in 2023 before reaching profitability by mid-2025.

An interactive AI Inference Economics Analyzer lets you model cost across 10 regions and 8 accelerator types.
