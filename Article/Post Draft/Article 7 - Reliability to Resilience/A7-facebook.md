# Facebook — Article 7: From Reliability to Resilience

New on ResistanceZero: From Reliability to Resilience.

A Tier rating describes infrastructure design. It does not describe how an operations team responds when that design is tested.

This article separates reliability, the probability of performing without failure, from resilience, the capacity to adapt, absorb and recover. It applies Hollnagel's four cornerstones of resilience engineering and builds a 7-dimension assessment plus a five-stage maturity model. The Uptime Institute's outage analysis, cited in the piece, attributes roughly 60-80% of outages to human error, process failures or organizational factors rather than equipment.

The case is a Tier III facility with 4.2 MW of IT load, 2N power distribution and N+1 chilled water cooling: highly reliable by design, and still exposed. It asks what the tier certificate does not measure.
