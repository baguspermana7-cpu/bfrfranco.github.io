# Facebook — Article 15: Data Center Service Catalog: 135 Services

New on ResistanceZero: Data Center Service Catalog: 135 Services.

What can a data center actually sell? This article catalogs 135 services across 11 categories and ranks them by revenue tier, with regional pricing for the Americas, Europe, Southeast Asia and Australia.

Colocation and hosting is the foundation, roughly 65-75% of industry revenue. A single 5MW wholesale deal at $120-220/kW/month generates $7.2M-$13.2M in annual recurring revenue. Advisory is project-based but commands $15K-$500K per engagement, in a consulting market valued at $9.8 billion in 2025 and growing 12% a year.

Each category is covered in turn, from consulting and commissioning to electrical, mechanical, monitoring, safety and compliance, managed operations, and emergency recovery. An interactive revenue calculator is included for operators and investors sizing a portfolio.
