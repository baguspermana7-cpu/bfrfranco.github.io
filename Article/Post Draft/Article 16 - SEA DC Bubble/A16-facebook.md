This article tests the numbers. Indonesia stands at 1,717 MW and climbing. The pipeline covers 135 upcoming facilities alongside 290 existing ones, and much of the new capacity is speculative, with tenants not yet secured.

It covers hyperscaler promises, stranded assets, historical parallels and what happens when the music stops. An interactive bubble risk calculator is included. Article 17 makes the opposing case.
