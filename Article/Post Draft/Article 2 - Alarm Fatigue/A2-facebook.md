# Facebook — Article 2: Alarm Fatigue Is Not a Human Problem

New on ResistanceZero: Alarm Fatigue Is Not a Human Problem.

When operators ignore alarms, the system has failed them, not the other way around. This article treats alarm fatigue as a system design failure and works through it with ISA-18.2, EEMUA 191, IEC 62682 and cognitive load theory.

ISA-18.2 defines an alarm flood as more than 10 alarms in 10 minutes for a single operator, and past that point human response capacity collapses. The case study is a live data center in the construction-to-operations transition, where a 10-week rationalization process, reviewing every alarm against defined engineering criteria, achieved greater than 90% alarm reduction. Results were measured over 90 days and compared against a 30-day baseline.

A taxonomy of alarm design failures and a calculator that compares your own alarm rates with ISA-18.2 benchmarks are included.
