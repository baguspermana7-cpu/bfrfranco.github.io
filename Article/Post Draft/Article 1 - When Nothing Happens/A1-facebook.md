# Facebook — Article 1: When Nothing Happens, Engineering Is Working

New on ResistanceZero: When Nothing Happens, Engineering Is Working.

On an uneventful data center shift, cooling stays within limits, the UPS is ready for a utility disturbance and deviations are investigated before they become incidents. None of it shows up in an incident report, and all of it is work.

This first article of the Engineering Journal shows how to document preventive work and make reliability visible beyond incident counts. It uses Hollnagel's Safety-I and Safety-II to explain why counting failures misses most of what keeps a site running, sets out an eight-stage proactive framework and a five-level maturity model, and draws on a 10 MW Tier III-equivalent facility that documented twelve prevented incidents in six months.

The Uptime Institute's 2024 outage analysis, cited in the piece, attributes over 55% of significant outages to operational and human factors. An interactive maturity calculator is included.
