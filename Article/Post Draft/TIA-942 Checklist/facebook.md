Something that comes up regularly in data center compliance conversations: a facility claims a Tier level, but when you actually walk through the TIA-942-B requirements systematically, you find gaps that the tier label does not capture.

TIA-942-B covers six domains — site and architecture, electrical, mechanical, telecom, fire protection, and security — with over 80 items in total. Most of the gaps I have seen in practice are not in the headline redundancy topology. They are in fire detection zone definition, cable pathway segregation, and access control documentation.

I built an interactive TIA-942 Compliance Checklist that walks through all 80-plus items with Tier 1-4 filtering and facility-type selection for enterprise, colo, hyperscale, edge, and modular sites. Electrical items are weighted at 1.5x because that is where most significant downtime events originate.

The tool is at resistancezero.com/tia-942-checklist.html. It runs entirely in your browser, nothing is transmitted.
