# LinkedIn — CX Calculator

**Char target**: ~1,400.

---

Shipped a Customer Experience calculator on resistancezero.com. Specific
to the data-center colocation / hyperscale operator audience.

The premise: most "CX" frameworks were built for SaaS. Bouncing them
into an industry where the customer is a procurement director negotiating
a fifteen-megawatt commitment over an eight-year horizon doesn't quite
work.

The CX Calculator works differently:

— Inputs in your language. Lease duration. Capacity ramp. SLA structure.
  Penalty / credit waterfall. Onboarding NPS proxy. Operations review
  cadence. Renewal probability.
— Outputs in your decision frame. Net retention. Expansion uplift.
  Renewal-risk index. Reference-ability score.
— Sensitivity grid baked in. What does a one-quarter slip in onboarding
  do to renewal probability? What does a missed SLA review do to
  reference-ability?

What it isn't: a Salesforce CX dashboard with extra steps. It's a
single-page decision tool for the conversation you have on the Monday
after a quarterly business review goes sideways.

PRO tier. Open methodology. Worth a play if you run a DC operations
account function and your quarterly reviews are turning into structured
arguments rather than aligned reviews.
