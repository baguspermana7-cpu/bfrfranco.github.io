# Facebook — Article 9: The HVAC Shock: Is Chiller-Free Cooling Possible in the Tropics?

New on ResistanceZero: The HVAC Shock: Is Chiller-Free Cooling Possible in the Tropics?.

When Nvidia talked about direct-to-chip cooling at CES 2026, Johnson Controls stock fell 7.5% and about $1.7B of market capitalization disappeared in hours. The market read "no chillers" as "no cooling".

This article separates the two. It explains what Nvidia actually announced, why a plant room still has to reject heat, and where tropical conditions limit chiller-free designs. A temperate city like Frankfurt gets 8-10 hours of free cooling a day in summer; Jakarta, with only an 8 C day-night swing, gets 2-4 hours of marginal free cooling.

It also walks through fault scenarios for a failing liquid-cooling loop, compares direct liquid cooling with traditional CRAC on a 10-year NPV, and lists the pros and cons. A PUE impact calculator is included.
