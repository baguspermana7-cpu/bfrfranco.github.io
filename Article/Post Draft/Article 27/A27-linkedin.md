# LinkedIn — Article 27: 20 Strategies to Solve the AI Data Center Workforce Crisis

Data center capacity plans need a staffing plan beside the power and cooling design. Article 27 puts numbers on why.

The shortfall is 467,000-498,000 unfilled operational positions worldwide (7x24 Exchange, MCGA). It is current, not forecast, in a sector adding capacity about 30% a year. Per AFCOM's 2024 report, 70% of data center personnel are 45 or older and 33% are at or near retirement. About 20,000 licensed electricians retire each year in the US alone, and electrical work is 45-70% of data center construction cost. McKinsey projects $6.7 trillion of cumulative investment through 2030, and that capital needs people to operate what it builds.

The article's first move is to split the problem. Physical operations cannot be done remotely. NOC and remote operations can be augmented by automation, and robots that replace technicians are 5-10 years from scale. Its second move is three levers: Create, Substitute, Extend, each with a different time to effect, from weeks to 7 years.

Then 20 strategies, with working examples such as Stack Infrastructure (about 50% of its workforce from college partnerships) and Equinix Pathways to Tech (2,000 students), and eight findings that 40+ sources agree on.

A free cost modeler lets you test the mix against your own facility size and budget.
