The DKI Jakarta + Banten corridor is the highest-density node on the Java-Bali backbone. At approximately 11.5 GW peak load, it is the dominant driver of transmission planning for the entire Jamali system.

What makes it unusual for grid analysis is the co-location of two completely different demand profiles: coastal and city-edge thermal generation serving a metropolitan load that includes 18-plus known data-center operators — all within reach of the same 500 and 150 kV switching infrastructure.

The provincial detail page adds the 20 kV DC-feeder layer to the standard transmission map so that layer is visible in its actual grid context.

---

What the page covers:

- Provincial headline statistics for DKI Jakarta + Banten: peak load, installed capacity, and DC operator count sourced from PLN Distribution UID Jakarta Raya + Banten 2024 baseline
- Named data-center operators with their 20 kV feeder connections
- 500/150 kV backbone with coastal coal and city-edge gas plant locations
- Provincial anchor substations and their role in the transmission topology
- Single-line diagram for the provincial network

---

The density of DC operators in this corridor creates a concentration risk that is not obvious when you are looking only at a facility's individual grid connection. A single 150 kV busbar failure at the right substation takes out multiple operators simultaneously. That topology is visible on the provincial map in a way that individual facility-level connection diagrams do not show.
