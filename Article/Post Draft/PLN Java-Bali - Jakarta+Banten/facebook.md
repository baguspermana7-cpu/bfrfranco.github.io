Something worth understanding if you are doing data center site selection or grid-resilience work in the Jakarta area: the concentration of DC operators in the Bekasi and Tangerang corridors means that "dual-feed from different substations" does not automatically mean independent feeds. Multiple substations in the metro area share common 150 kV upstream infrastructure.

The Jakarta + Banten provincial grid detail page shows this topology. It adds a 20 kV DC-feeder overlay — with named operators and their feeder connection points — to the full 500/150 kV transmission map. Peak load is approximately 11.5 GW from PLN's 2024 baseline. There are 18-plus known DC operators mapped in the corridor.

You can see it at resistancezero.com/pln-java-grid-jakarta-banten.html.
