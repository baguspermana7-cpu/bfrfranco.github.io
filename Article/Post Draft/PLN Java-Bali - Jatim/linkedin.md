East Java carries the heaviest single thermal block on the Indonesian power system: PLTU Paiton at 4.71 GW on the Probolinggo coast feeds the eastern 500 kV ring, while PLTGU Gresik at 1.58 GW anchors the Surabaya metropolitan load.

The Jawa Timur grid is also the landing point for the Java-Bali submarine interconnect — the physical cable that ties the two islands into a single synchronous system and makes Bali's grid stability dependent on East Java's dispatch state.

The industrial load diversity in East Java is worth noting for grid-stability analysis. The petrochemical sector at Gresik and Tuban runs large continuous process loads. Cement at Tuban and Gresik adds another baseload industrial profile. That combination, alongside the Surabaya DC cluster's largely variable load, creates a load composition that is different from the Jakarta corridor's more DC-heavy mix.
