# LinkedIn — Geopolitics 1: The 72-Hour Warning

More than 20 nations now ask their citizens to be ready to manage on their own for 72 hours or longer. Why now?

The Geopolitics 1 article compares the guidance and the disruptions behind it. NEW START expired on February 5, 2026, so for the first time since 1972 there are no limits on US and Russian nuclear arsenals. Baltic cables and pipelines have been damaged since 2023, and the war in Ukraine has shown that power, communications and water systems can be systematically degraded.

The country table is revealing. Sweden asks for 1 week and has about 65,000 shelters (7M capacity); Finland asks for 72 hours with 50,000+ shelters (4.5M capacity). Germany recommends 10 days but has 579 functional shelters for 83 million people, down from 2,000+ at the Cold War peak. Poland made 72 hours mandatory by law in 2024. Indonesia has no specific civil defense guidance.

What the guidance recommends is practical: 3 liters of water per person per day, non-perishable food, a hand-crank radio, cash, and a 2-week supply of prescription medication. The article's conclusion is that preparedness is not panic; it is resilience.
