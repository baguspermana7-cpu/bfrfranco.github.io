# X / Twitter — Geopolitics 1: The 72-Hour Warning (thread)

1/ More than 20 nations now tell their citizens to be able to survive 72 hours on their own. A thread on what the official guidance says and why governments are issuing it now. 🧵

2/ The context: NEW START expired on February 5, 2026. For the first time since 1972 there are no limits on US and Russian nuclear arsenals. Baltic cables and pipelines have been damaged since 2023, and NATO credibility has been questioned.

3/ The guidance differs by country. Sweden: 1 week. Finland: 72 hours minimum. Germany: 10 days. Taiwan: 1 week. Japan: 2 weeks of shelter capacity. Poland made 72 hours a legal mandate in its 2024 civil defense law.

4/ Shelters differ even more. Sweden has about 65,000 shelters (7M capacity), Finland 50,000+ (4.5M capacity), South Korea 17,000+ public shelters.

5/ Germany is the gap case: 579 functional shelters for 83 million people, down from 2,000+ at the Cold War peak. That leaves 99%+ of citizens without shelter access.

6/ Indonesia and ASEAN have no specific civil defense guidance; the focus is natural disasters only, with no civil defense shelter network.

7/ What the guidance actually asks for is modest: at least 3 liters of drinking water per person per day, non-perishable food, a battery or hand-crank radio, cash because ATMs may fail, and a 2-week supply of prescription medication.

8/ The authors' reading: preparedness is not panic. Governments are not predicting imminent catastrophe; they are acknowledging that infrastructure can be degraded and the risk environment has changed.
