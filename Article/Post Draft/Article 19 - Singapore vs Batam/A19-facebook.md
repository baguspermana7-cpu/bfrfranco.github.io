# Facebook — Article 19: Singapore vs Batam Data Centers: Why Cost Alone Doesn't Win

New on ResistanceZero: Singapore vs Batam Data Centers: Why Cost Alone Doesn't Win.

Every morning a fast ferry crosses 20 kilometers of strait between Singapore and Batam in 35 minutes. Batam land costs far less, yet global providers keep choosing Singapore.

The numbers are lopsided. In Singapore, land costs 40-100x more, electricity runs 2-3x higher and labor is 4-5x pricier. But Singapore's DC-CFA2 framework (December 2025) allocated 200+ MW of new capacity with strict requirements: PUE 1.25 maximum and 50% green energy, and growth is physically capped by land and power.

This article gives a decision matrix: latency-sensitive interconnect stays in Singapore, while AI training, storage and disaster recovery move to Batam where power and land are. It also describes the corridor model that uses both, with an outlook to 2030.
