# Facebook — Article 6: Post-Incident RCA Fails Without Design Authority

New on ResistanceZero: Post-Incident RCA Fails Without Design Authority.

Root cause analysis is the most widely practiced post-incident discipline in critical infrastructure, yet recurrence stays high. This article argues the failure is organizational, not analytical: RCA without design authority produces documentation, not system change.

According to the Uptime Institute's 2023 survey, about 60% of significant incidents have a contributing factor that an earlier RCA identified but did not effectively address. The article reviews five RCA methods, explains why a 95% completion rate with 45% implementation and 35% recurrence is compliance rather than learning, and borrows the design authority concept from the nuclear industry (IAEA GSR Part 2, 2016).

It proposes an RCA-to-design pipeline and includes an interactive canvas showing how design authority level changes recurrence.
