# PLN Jawa Timur Provincial Grid

## Provincial Stats

## Headline Anchor

**PLTU Paiton complex 1-9** — coal — **4,710 MW** — Probolinggo. Largest single-site coal generation in Indonesia. Operators: Indonesia Power (1-2), Paiton Energy (3+7+8), POMI/IPMOMI (5+6), Jawa Power (9). Commissioned 1994.

## Other Power Plant Anchors

- **PLTGU Gresik** — gas — 1,578 MW — operated by PJB (1993)
- **PLTGU Grati** — gas — 865 MW — Pasuruan (1997)
- **PLTU Pacitan** — coal — 630 MW — south coast (2013)
- **PLTU Tanjung Awar-Awar** — coal — 700 MW — Tuban (2012)

## Java-Bali Submarine Cable (CRITICAL)

- **4 × 150 kV cables** between Cable Head Ketapang (Java) ↔ Cable Head Gilimanuk (Bali)
- **~340 MW** total transfer capacity
- Commissioned 1989-1996 in stages
- 35 km strait crossing
- 275 kV upgrade is **planned but NOT yet commissioned**
- Source attribution `pln-jbi-submarine` in dataset edges
