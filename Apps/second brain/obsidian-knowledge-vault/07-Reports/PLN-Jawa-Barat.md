# PLN Jawa Barat Provincial Grid

Province sub-page with renewable focus. CSS prefix `pjgjb-`. Map fitBounds `[[-7.8, 106.0], [-6.0, 108.8]]`.

## Provincial Stats

- **Peak load**: 8,200 MW
- **Reserve margin**: 28 %
- **Renewable mix**: 34 % (highest in Java due to hydro + geothermal)
- **Hydro share**: 22 %
- **DC operators**: 6 (smaller than Jakarta cluster)
- **DC capacity**: 90 MW IT load

## Renewable Anchors

- **PLTA Cirata** — hydro — 1,008 MW (largest hydro plant in Java) + 145 MWp floating PV (2023)
- **PLTA Saguling** — hydro — 700 MW (1985)
- **PLTP Wayang Windu** — geothermal — 227 MW (Pangalengan)
- **PLTP Gunung Salak** — geothermal — 377 MW (Bogor)
- **PLTP Darajat** — geothermal — 270 MW (Garut)
- **PLTP Patuha** — geothermal — 55 MW (Ciwidey)
- **PLTP Kamojang** — geothermal — 235 MW (Garut, Indonesia's first geothermal plant, 1982)

## Coal Anchors

- PLTU Cirebon 1+2 — supercritical — 1,660 MW (Cirebon Electric Power)
- PLTU Indramayu — coal — 990 MW (Indonesia Power)
- PLTU Pelabuhan Ratu — coal — 1,050 MW (Sukabumi south coast)

## Industrial Intakes

- KIIC Karawang
- Suryacipta Karawang Timur
- Indotaisei Purwakarta
- KBI Purwakarta
- Cirebon Power industrial estate
- Bandung Timur edge cluster

## Key Substations

- GITET Cirata — 500 kV, 2,000 MVA, 1988 (PLTA evacuation)
- GITET Bandung Selatan — 500 kV, 3,000 MVA, 2000
- GITET Bandung Utara — 500 kV, 3,000 MVA, 2008 (Wayang Windu evacuation)
- GITET Cibatu — 500 kV, 4,000 MVA, 2001 (shared with [[PLN-Jakarta-Banten]])
- GITET Tasikmalaya — 500 kV, 1,500 MVA, 2010
- GITET Mandirancan — 500 kV, 2,000 MVA, 1998
