What is a CDU? A Coolant Distribution Unit hydraulically decouples the facility water loop from the chip-side technology cooling loop and regulates flow, temperature and pressure to direct-to-chip cold plates. This toolkit is an independent, educational reference built from published vendor datasheets and standards (ASHRAE TC 9.9, OCP, DMTF Redfish) — every figure is source-tagged, and links are validated. Not procurement, safety, or engineering advice.
