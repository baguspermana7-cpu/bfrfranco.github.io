PLN Sumatra interconnected grid and the Batam island system

The Sumatra 275/150 kV system, a north-south backbone from Aceh through Medan, Pekanbaru and Palembang to Lampung, plus the separate Batam-Bintan (Barelang) island grid run by PLN Batam, where Medco Power operates the Panaran gas plants.

02 What the dataset holds

Batam is islanded. The Batam-Bintan-Rempang-Galang (Barelang) 150 kV grid is not synchronously tied to the mainland Sumatra interconnection — it is its own system run by b'right PLN Batam, with Medco Power's Panaran gas plants and PLTU Tanjung Kasam as base-load, and the Nongsa Digital Park subsea-cable DC hub, a Singapore-adjacent AI/edge load.
