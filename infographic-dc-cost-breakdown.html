Data-centre build cost by category where $10M goes, and what each tier adds

Mechanical and cooling is the largest share of a build, ahead of electrical. The split of a $10M reference build, the tier multipliers and the cost facts behind them.

01 Where the money goes

A $10M reference build split by category; the shares add to 100 %.

CategoryShareOf $10MRelative to the largestWhat it covers
Mechanical / Cooling28 %$2.8MThe single largest cost category. Includes chillers, cooling towers, CRAH/CRAC units, piping systems, hot/cold aisle containment, and BMS integration. For AI/HPC facilities requiring liquid cooling, this can rise to 35%+ of total cost.
Electrical22 %$2.2MUPS systems, PDUs, switchgear, transformers, generators, and electrical distribution. High-density AI racks require 3-5x more electrical capacity per rack, pushing this category higher.
IT Infrastructure18 %$1.8MRacks, cabling (structured copper and fiber), network switches, patch panels, and cable management. AI deployments add GPU servers, high-speed interconnects, and specialized networking.
Construction / Civil15 %$1.5MBuilding shell, raised floor or slab, structural reinforcement, roof and walls, loading docks, and site work. Modular/prefab construction can reduce this by 20-30%.
Fire Suppression5 %$0.5MClean agent (Novec/FM-200) systems, VESDA early detection, pre-action sprinklers, and gas detection. Code requirements vary by jurisdiction.
Security4 %$0.4MAccess control, CCTV, mantraps, biometric readers, fencing, bollards, and security operations center (SOC) infrastructure. Hyperscalers spend 2-3x on physical security.
Commissioning3 %$0.3MIntegrated systems testing (IST), Level 4/5 commissioning, performance verification, and documentation. Critical for Tier III/IV certification — never skip this.
Contingency5 %$0.5MBudget reserve for scope changes, supply chain delays, permitting issues, and unforeseen conditions. Best practice is 10-15%, but 5% is the minimum baseline.

03 Cost facts

Cooling = Largest Single Cost

At 28% of total CAPEX, mechanical/cooling is the largest single line item. It exceeds even electrical infrastructure (22%) and determines long-term OPEX efficiency.

AI Racks Cost 3x Traditional

A traditional 5-10 kW rack costs ~$15K to deploy. An AI/HPC rack at 40-132+ kW costs $45K-$80K due to liquid cooling, high-density power, and reinforced infrastructure.

5% Contingency Is Not Enough

Industry best practice recommends 10-15% contingency for greenfield builds. Supply chain delays, permitting issues, and scope changes routinely consume the 5% baseline.
