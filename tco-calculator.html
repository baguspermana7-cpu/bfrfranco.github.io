Method

  • Build CAPEXP_IT (MW) · regional cost/MW · tier multiplier (II 0.85, III 1.0, IV 1.35)
  • Build OPEXpower P_IT · PUE · 8,760 h · $/kWh + staff · salary + maintenance 3 % and insurance 0.7 % of CAPEX + network $20k/MW/mo + 4 % overhead
  • ColocationP_IT (kW) · wholesale $/kW/mo · 12 + setup $50k/MW + 1.5 FTE/MW + network $15k/MW/mo + 3 % overhead, escalated yearly
  • CloudP_IT (kW) · $420/kW/mo · 12 · (1 − discount) · (1 + 15 % egress + 10 % support + 2 % other), +2.5 %/yr
  • Growthcapacity compounds at the growth rate; costs inflate per category each year
  • Breakevenfirst year cumulative build ≤ cumulative colocation
