How to use

  1. Download a sample fixture above or use your own backup file from the device.
  2. In the lab, click a module (Relay EPZ, UPS RPT, or ATS Simulator) from the left nav.
  3. Drop or click the upload zone to import your file. Files are processed locally — nothing is uploaded.
  4. Review decoded settings, incident classifications, or ATS sequence. Use Export Excel to create a maintenance evidence workbook.
  5. For EPZ diff review: import both before and after files to see protection setting changes and risk flags.
