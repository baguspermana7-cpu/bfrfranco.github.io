How to read this. Every figure is derived by the fire engine (js/fire-engine.js, 31/31 tests) from your inputs. Clean-agent mass uses the NFPA 2001 equation W = (V/s)·(C/(100−C)); inert agents use ln(100/(100−C)). A word appears beside a figure only when it needs attention: watch near a limit, hazard past one. Occupant safety: the design concentration must stay at or below the agent NOAEL. Li-ion: the off-gas of a battery in thermal runaway far exceeds the flammable limit of any room, so early H₂/CO off-gas detection (UL 9540A, NFPA 855) and ventilation come before suppression. Cost-class values are illustrative. Not a substitute for a fire-protection engineer's stamped design.
