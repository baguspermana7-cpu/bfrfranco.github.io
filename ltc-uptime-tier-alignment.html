Topology & maintainability

Pick a Tier, then take blocks out. The sweep tests every element under planned maintenance and under failure.

Tier I – IVConcurrent maintainabilityFault toleranceSimplified power & cooling paths
Sweep — every element out, one at a time

Maintenance is planned, so an alternate path may be switched in first. Failure is not, so only paths already carrying load count. Switch between the power and cooling paths; both face the same two tests. Compartmentalisation (Tier IV) is not modelled.

On percentages. The figures often quoted per Tier (99.671 %, 99.741 %, 99.982 %, 99.995 %) are not part of the Tier Standard: Topology, which defines Tiers by the objectives above. Availability depends on operations as much as topology — compute it for your own blocks in the Availability tab.

Fuel and engines. Every Tier requires at least 12 hours of on-site fuel. For Tier III and IV, on-site engine generators are the primary source and must be rated to carry the site without a runtime limit at the rating used.

Certification. Tier Certification of Design Documents (TCDD), of Constructed Facility (TCCF) and of Operational Sustainability are issued by Uptime Institute only; a self-declared “Tier III design” is not a certification.
